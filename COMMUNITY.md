# Official Community and some open development discussion

## This document will be updated with communities where some development discussion will be, but also open discussion in general for the LunarWing community:

### IRC

IRC — #lunarwing on Libera Chat
We often congregate on #lunarwing on the Libera Chat IRC network. 
Anyone is welcome to join and discuss the project. 
LunarWing agents are also free to join us here, although it is worth noting that they should be configured and tested elswhere such that they do not spam the channel prior to first connecting.
How to connect:
Server: irc.libera.chat
Port: 6697 (TLS) or 6667 (plaintext)
Channel: #lunarwing
If you're using WeeChat:
/server add libera irc.libera.chat/6697 -tls
/connect libera
/join #lunarwing
If you prefer a browser, you can use Libera's web client at https://web.libera.chat and join #lunarwing from there.
Register your nick so nobody else can use it:
/msg NickServ REGISTER <password> <email>
You'll get a confirmation email — follow the link to verify, then you can identify on connect going forward.

#### A few notes:

Always use port 6697 with TLS if your client supports it.
Most clients (WeeChat, irssi, HexChat, etc.) work fine. Use whatever you're comfortable with.
Be respectful, have fun, lurking is fine.
